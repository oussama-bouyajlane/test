Model Description

FMMM — Fuzzy Meta-Maturity Model for assessing Industry 4.0, Industry 5.0, and Circular Economy in Manufacturing.

Paradigm Architecture
INDUSTRY 4.0
Digital transformation through automation, IIoT, big data, AI/ML, cyber-physical systems, and smart manufacturing.
INDUSTRY 5.0
Human-centric manufacturing — wellbeing, resilience, sustainability co-centered with technological advancement.
CIRCULAR ECONOMY
Regenerative systems — ecodesign, waste valorization, material loops, energy transition, and LCA integration.
ISM-MICMAC Taxonomy
L1 — DRIVERS
D1, D6
High driving power, low dependence. Underpin the entire system.
L2 — ENABLERS
D3, D4, D5, D9
High driving and dependence. Bridge between strategy and execution.
L3 — EXECUTION
D2, D7
Operational dimensions enabled by higher levels.
L4 — OUTCOMES
D8, D10
Dependent dimensions expressing achieved maturity.
